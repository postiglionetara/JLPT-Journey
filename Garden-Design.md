# 🌸 The Grammar Garden

## The Story

Hidden behind a quiet path of cherry blossom trees is the Grammar Garden.

Every time a learner truly understands a new grammar point, the garden becomes a little warmer.

Cats are drawn to peaceful places.

As you continue learning, new cats decide to visit your garden.

By the end of your journey, your garden is full of friends you've met through your dedication.
